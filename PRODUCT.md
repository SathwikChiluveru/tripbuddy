# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Independent travelers who want to meet and connect with people while planning or taking a trip.

## Product Purpose

TripBuddy helps travelers discover small group trips, host plans, join trips, and coordinate with their travel group. Success measures have not yet been defined.

## Positioning

TripBuddy makes it easier for independent travelers to find people to connect with as they travel, by bringing trip discovery and group conversation together around a planned journey.

## Operating Context

Travelers can browse trips publicly, then sign in to host or join one, maintain a profile with visited and planned destinations, and chat with their trip group. Google is the intended sign-in flow; OAuth credentials must be configured for it to work. Local demonstration itineraries are labeled as demos.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Trip discovery, search, and categories; trip hosting, joining, editing, and deletion.
- Traveler profiles, including visited and planned countries.
- Session-authorized group chat using Socket.IO.
- The current project uses React, Express, MongoDB, and session authentication.
- Demo data belongs only to the explicitly named local development database. Do not alter hosted data or deploy unless the user asks.
- Product success measures and additional differentiators remain open for future discussion.

## Brand Commitments

The product is named TripBuddy. The user likes the current website theme and wants it to remain recognizable while individual designs, including trip cards and chat, continue to improve.

## Evidence on Hand

- The repository contains local destination photography and labeled local demo trips.
- No verified testimonials, customer outcomes, or real community activity are established by the repository. Do not present demo content as real traveler activity.

## Product Principles

- Help independent travelers find companionship around a trip.
- Make it clear how to discover, join, host, and coordinate a trip.
- Keep traveler profiles and group conversation connected to the trips they support.
- Represent local demo content honestly and preserve the boundary around hosted data.
